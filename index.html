Chalamate

Chalamate is a personal, Russian-speaking assistant used through Telegram. When its owner explicitly asks to save a thought, Chalamate can add that thought and related organization fields to a designated Google Sheets Inbox in the owner's Google Drive.

The Google connection is limited to files available to Chalamate through Google's drive.file permission. It is used for the designated Inbox feature.
